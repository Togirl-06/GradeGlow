# GradeGlow Life — Game Design

**Loop:** Learn → Practice → Graduate → Get hired → Work → Get promoted → Build your life.

## World
Isometric 2.5D city, 61×21 tiles, 10 districts (residential, university, hospital, corporate, tech,
courthouse, shopping, financial, employment center, entertainment), 20 enterable buildings with
interiors, interactables and NPCs. Day/night cycle with building opening hours. Tap-to-walk or WASD/arrows;
E interact, P phone, M map.

## Life simulation
Needs (0–100): energy, hunger, happiness, health, social, hygiene, and stress (shown as "Calm").
Rent and bills every 30 days, groceries, transit pass, housing tiers, furniture comfort, savings, debt.
Relationships with NPCs grow only through conversations (diminishing returns within a day).

## University
Degrees: Computer Science, Nursing, Accounting, Business (associate after 3 courses), Law and Medicine
(need a bachelor's first). Per course: enrol → 4 lectures (weekdays 9–5) → 240 study minutes → exam
(fee, 75% to pass). GPA on a 4.0 scale; graduation invitation → attend the ceremony on the University
stage → game certificate (honours at GPA ≥ 3.4). Certificates are game items, **not accredited**.

## Careers
Software Engineer, Nurse, Doctor, Lawyer, Accountant, Business Manager. Ranks Intern → Director.
Job board → résumé (AI assistant) → application scored on qualifications → invite by email/SMS →
playable interview (7 scored dimensions) → offer/waitlist/reject with advice → contract → negotiation →
workplace tasks (six stage kinds) → payday, performance, promotion, layoffs, resignation, new employer, own company.

## Safety framing
Medical and legal content is an educational simulation in the fictional jurisdiction "Glowland". It
is not advice and completing it grants no real licence.

## Later additions
- **Needs:** hygiene (shower at home) and stress (reduced by sleep, raised by overwork); stress >70 lowers work readiness.
- **NPC schedules:** weekdays 09–12 and 13–17 NPCs work within 2 tiles of their post, 12–13 break (radius 4), otherwise rest; chat shows their current activity.
- **Lectures:** attend a lecture to see the professor's board with highlighted organs/terms, ask questions, take a check quiz for up to +30 study minutes.
- **Hiring outcomes:** offer, reject, waitlist, second (harder) interview, or request for more information with a deadline.
- **Pharmacist career:** Bachelor of Pharmacy (5 courses), Dispensing Assistant certificate, hospital/clinic pharmacy stations, dose and reorder-point calculations.
